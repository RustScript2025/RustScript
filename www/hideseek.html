🔍 HIDE AND SEEK 🔍

A RustScript Adventure Game

How to Play

Find all your hidden friends before you run out of turns!

Movement

N S E W - Directions

U D - Up/Down (Hallway only)

Actions

L - Look around for hiding spots

1-9 - Search a hiding spot

H - Get hint (free, then costs pts)

M - Show map

Q - Quit game

Scoring by Difficulty

Easy: +100 find, -50 miss

Medium: +75 find, -75 miss

Hard: +50 find, -100 miss

Hints by Difficulty

Easy: 3 free, then -25 pts

Medium: 2 free, then -50 pts

Hard: 1 free, then -75 pts
