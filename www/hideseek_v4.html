How to Play

Find all your hidden friends before you run out of turns!

Movement

N S E W - Cardinal directions

U D - Up/Down (Hallway only)

Actions

L - Look around for hiding spots

1-9 - Search a hiding spot

H - Get hint (free, then costs pts)

M - Show map

Q - Quit game

Scoring

Easy: +100 find, -50 miss, 3 free hints

Medium: +75 find, -75 miss, 2 free hints

Hard: +50 find, -100 miss, 1 free hint
